INCIDENT-2088: Billing invoice export fails intermittently under load

Status: RESOLVED
Component: billing
Severity: MEDIUM
Date: 2026-03-22

Summary:
The invoice export in the billing portal intermittently failed during
end-of-month peak load. Users saw a spinner that never resolved. The export
itself was a PDF batch job, unrelated to CSV reporting.

Root cause:
The billing export held a database connection for the full duration of the PDF
render. During end-of-month peak, the connection pool was exhausted, and new
export requests waited on a connection until they timed out client-side. This
was a connection-pool contention problem, not a query-performance problem.

Resolution:
Fixed in v2.4.3. Moved PDF rendering to an async worker that releases the
database connection before rendering. Raised the pool size for the billing
service from 10 to 25.

Follow-up:
Added connection-pool saturation metrics to the billing dashboard.
